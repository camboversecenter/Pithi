# About PITHI

PITHI ("ceremony" in Khmer) is a free, community-built platform for planning and
managing traditional Cambodian ceremonies — connecting hosts, organizers, and
vendors in one Khmer-language marketplace. This page introduces the team behind
PITHI and the partners who make it possible.
